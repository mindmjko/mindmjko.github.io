Welcome to theMagnetic & Intelligent Nanomaterials Design Lab

Our Mission

To advance precision healthcare by engineering nanoarchitectures for personalized and adaptive control over disease.

At MIND Lab, we investigate how nanoscale structure, interfaces, and architecture shape material behavior and interactions with biological systems. We use these insights to design nanomaterials with programmable responses to external stimuli (e.g., magnetic fields and light) and explore how their responses can adapt to changing biological conditions.

By connecting nanoarchitecture design, remote actuation, and biomedical imaging, we aim to increase the degree of control over where, when, and how materials act across molecular, cellular, and tissue scales.

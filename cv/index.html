Profile

Min Jun Ko develops multifunctional nanomaterials by connecting precise synthesis, structural control, advanced characterization, and data-guided design. His work focuses on materials whose magnetic, optical, catalytic, and biomedical functions can be deliberately programmed and remotely controlled.
